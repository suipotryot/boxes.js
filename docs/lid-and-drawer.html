Lid & Drawer

Two optional, independent additions to a box: a fixed lid, and a sliding drawer sleeve.

Fixed lid

Enable the lid in the left panel's Lid section. Its position is set with a single field:

  • Insertion height — the height at which the lid's underside sits inside the box. A lower value seats the lid flush with, or recessed below, the top of the walls; a higher value would sit above it.

The value is validated against a minimum and maximum for the current box; if it's out of range, use the Auto-fix button to snap it back into a valid position.

Because the lid needs a level rim to rest on, overriding one outer wall's height (see The Grid Model) applies to the whole outer perimeter, not just that one segment.

Drawer sleeve

Enable the drawer in the left panel's Drawer box section. This builds a second, independent enclosing box around your current box's footprint — always five pieces (base, lid, and three walls) — open on one side, so the box you designed slides out of it like a drawer.

FieldMeaning
Open sideWhich side of the sleeve is left open: top, bottom, right, or left.
Play / marginSliding clearance between the inner box and the sleeve — applied on the closed side of the opening axis, and on both sides of the other axis. The open side itself stays flush, with no extra clearance.
Wood thicknessMaterial thickness for the sleeve, set independently from the main box's outer thickness.

Tip

Add a grip notch on the open side of your inner box so there's something to pull it out by.
