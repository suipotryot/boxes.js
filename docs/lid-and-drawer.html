Lid & Drawer

Optional, independent additions to a box: a lid on top, horizontal panels sunk into the walls, and a sliding drawer sleeve.

Lid on top

Tick Lid on top in the left panel's Lid section: a flat lid rests on the walls' own top edge, which add fingers to receive it — like the base plate, mirrored. It adds its own thickness to the box's total height.

Because the lid needs a level rim to rest on, overriding one outer wall's height (see The Grid Model) applies to the whole outer perimeter, not just that one segment.

Horizontal panels

The Horizontal panels section adds as many flat panels as you need, each sunk into the outer walls at its own height: a shelf, a recessed lid, or just a strip along one wall. Each panel's tabs fit into a row of mortises cut mid-height into the outer walls. Click Add a horizontal panel, then set its Height — the height at which its underside rests, measured from the bottom of the box. A new panel starts as high as allowed.

The height is validated for the current box: above the tallest divider, and low enough for the panel to stay inside the walls — one thickness lower when the lid is on, so its mortises leave wood under the lid's fingers. If it's out of range, use the Auto-fix button to snap it back into a valid position. Make sure two panels (or a panel and a recessed base plate) are far enough apart for their mortises not to merge: the app does not check it.

A project saved with a recessed lid opens with that lid turned into a horizontal panel, holes included.

Partial coverage

A horizontal panel can cover only a strip along one wall: tick Partial coverage, pick the Strip side and set its Strip depth, measured from the inner face of that wall. The rest of the box stays open — handy for engraving a label that reads from above without getting in the way of the contents.

The wall the strip leans against keeps its full row of mortises, the two side walls only keep the ones under the strip, and the opposite wall has none. If the strip is too narrow for a tab on its short sides (it must be wider than two finger margins), a hint says so: the strip is then only held by the wall it leans against.

Drawer sleeve

Enable the drawer in the left panel's Drawer box section. This builds a second, independent enclosing box around your current box's footprint — always five pieces (base, lid, and three walls) — open on one side, so the box you designed slides out of it like a drawer.

FieldMeaning
Open sideWhich side of the sleeve is left open: top, bottom, right, or left.
Play / marginSliding clearance between the inner box and the sleeve — applied on the closed side of the opening axis, and on both sides of the other axis. The open side itself stays flush, with no extra clearance.
Wood thicknessMaterial thickness for the sleeve, set independently from the main box's outer thickness.

Tip

Add a grip notch on the open side of your inner box so there's something to pull it out by.
